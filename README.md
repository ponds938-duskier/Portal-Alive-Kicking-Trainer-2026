## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use movement options sparingly in chambers that depend on momentum, portals, buttons, cubes, or precise launches. Speed, air-control, low-gravity, and no-clip settings can alter intended puzzle solutions, so disable them when you want the standard physics experience.

### Tools & Inventory

Portal: Alive & Kicking has no conventional inventory or resource-management system. The primary tool is the portal device, while cubes, switches, buttons, turrets, and other chamber objects are handled directly in the environment. The trainer therefore focuses on movement, survival, saves, and chamber conditions rather than ammunition or item quantities.

### World & Environment

The game retells the original Aperture Science testing experience through updated Portal 2 visuals, revised presentation, improved puzzle cues, and escape areas. Use quick save before experimenting with portal routes or entering hazardous sections, and reload after testing trainer options that affect chamber behavior.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat is limited and secondary to puzzle solving. Turrets and environmental hazards can threaten the player, but the game does not use a conventional weapon, ammunition, or combat progression system. Invulnerability, turret freezing, and hazard controls are intended to make these encounters safer without introducing new weapons.
